🔒 Password Security Policy

Passwords require minimum 8 characters with at least 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 numeric digit, and 1 special symbol. Passwords are salted and hashed using SHA-256 before persistence.

🛡️ 5-Attempt Lockout Mechanism

Accounts are automatically locked out for 60 seconds after 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts. All failed and locked attempts are written to the immutable audit trail.

🔑 Mandatory First Login Change

Users provisioned by the Principal or issued a temporary password reset must update their credentials on first login before accessing their dashboard features.
